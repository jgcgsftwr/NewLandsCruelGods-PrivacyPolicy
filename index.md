# PRIVACY POLICY FOR "NEW LANDS AND CRUEL GODS"

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This privacy policy explains how New Lands and Cruel Gods (the "App") handles data. The App is a turn-based strategy game that runs entirely on your device.

## 1. DATA WE COLLECT

We do not collect any personal data. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The App never transmits any data to the internet or to any server operated by us.

## 2. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The App stores data only in private, app-specific storage on your device:

* Game save files (maps, cities, armies, and match state)
* Arcade campaign progress
* Settings you choose (such as your player display name, sound, and gameplay preferences)
* Local achievement system

This data is used solely to run the App and to let you continue your games. It is not shared with anyone.

## 3. LOCAL NETWORK MULTIPLAYER

The App includes local area network (LAN) multiplayer, in which several devices on the same Wi-Fi network play a single shared match. To make this work, the App requests the Internet and Access Local Network permissions.

When LAN multiplayer is in use, the following information is exchanged directly between the participating devices on your local network:

* The player display name you entered in Settings (or a default name such as "Player 1", or "Aspiring Leader")
* Game data (map, moves, units, and other match information)

No information is sent to the internet, to any server, or to any device that has not joined the match. LAN multiplayer only works between devices connected to the same local network.

## 4. PERMISSIONS

* **INTERNET and ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK:** used only for LAN multiplayer, as described above. The App does not use these permissions for any other purpose.

## 5. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

The App does not share, sell, or disclose any data to third parties. No advertising or analytics services are included in the App.

## 6. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Data stored by the App remains on your device until you delete it. You can:

* Delete individual save files in-game, or
* Uninstall the App, which removes App data from your device.

## 7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

The App does not require an account or any online service. However, any user of the App, including a child, will have data stored locally on their own device: save files, arcade progress, achievements and the player display name they enter. In addition, during local network multiplayer, that display name and match data are shared with the other devices taking part in the same match on the same local network, as described in section 3.

If a child is using the App, a parent or guardian may review and delete this data by deleting save files in-game, or by uninstalling the App, which removes App data from the device.

## 8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy. Any changes will be posted in this document with an updated revision date.
